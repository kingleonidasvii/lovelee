lovelee — Berlin indie pop artist

lovelee is the artist project of Leon Byvanck, a Berlin-based singer-songwriter and guitarist making indie pop and bedroom pop. Positioned between Steve Lacy and Clairo, lovelee's music is guitar-driven, emotionally direct, and modern without being nostalgic. His GEMA-registered catalogue has accumulated over 800,000 streams. He is releasing the Storm Chasers EP in June 2026 and the Violet EP across the second half of 2026. Alongside his solo work, he is the touring guitarist for Berlin indie act Dilla, with over 150 concerts at major festivals including Hurricane, Southside, and Superbloom, and television appearances on ProSieben, MDR, ARD, and ZDF. His singles Romeo, Demons, Unahana, and Tears are available on all streaming platforms.
