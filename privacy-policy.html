last updated may 2026

who runs this site

Leon Byvanck (lovelee), based in Germany. Questions? management@itsloveleee.com — or see the Impressum.

what is collected

This site only collects data when you actively do something:

If you sign up for the newsletter, your email is stored with Brevo (France) and used only to send updates. Unsubscribe anytime via the link in any email.

If you send a message via the contact form, your name, email, and message are forwarded to the site owner via Web3Forms (me, Leon Byvanck). Nothing is stored beyond what's needed to reply.

If you accept cookies, a Meta Pixel loads to help understand how people find the site. This is optional and only happens with your consent.

A cookie preference is saved in your browser to remember your choice. That's the only thing stored without asking.

third-party services

Depending on what you do, your data may pass through: Brevo (newsletter), Web3Forms (contact form), Meta (analytics, if consented), and Cloudflare (newsletter infrastructure).

your rights

Under GDPR you can request access to, correction, or deletion of your data at any time. Email management@itsloveleee.com. You can also file a complaint with the BfDI (Germany's data protection authority).
