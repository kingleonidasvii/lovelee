privacy policy

last updated may 2026

who is responsible

This website is operated by Leon Byvanck (lovelee), Essener Str. 20, 10555 Berlin, Germany. For any data-related questions, contact management@itsloveleee.com. See also the Impressum.

what data is collected

This site collects data in the following ways:

  • Cookies — a small file is stored in your browser to remember your cookie preference. No other cookies are set without your consent.
  • Analytics — if you consent, a tracking pixel (Meta Pixel) is loaded to help understand how visitors find and interact with the site. This data is processed by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.
  • Newsletter — if you sign up, your email address is stored and processed by Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) to send you updates. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email.
  • Contact form — if you send a message, your name, email, and message content are submitted via Web3Forms and forwarded to the site owner's email. This data is not stored beyond what is necessary to respond.

legal basis

  • Cookie consent is based on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25 TDDDG (your explicit consent).
  • Newsletter processing is based on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your explicit sign-up).
  • Contact form processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (responding to your inquiry).

third parties

  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. — analytics via Meta Pixel (only if consented). Meta privacy policy.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS) — newsletter delivery. Brevo privacy policy.
  • Web3Forms — contact form submission. Web3Forms privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare — the newsletter endpoint is hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare privacy policy.

your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, as well as the right to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email management@itsloveleee.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Germany, the relevant authority is the Bundesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit (BfDI).

cookies

To withdraw your cookie consent, clear your browser's local storage for this site and reload the page — the consent banner will reappear.
